# Pannier

A temporary link queue for your Mac menu bar.

Pannier is a small menu bar app that holds links you'll get to later — a tab you'd otherwise leave open in Safari, an article you want to read tonight, a docs page you keep coming back to. Copy a link, hit ⌃⌘L, and it's saved. Open the menu bar icon to pick something to read.

Links auto-expire (default 7 days, configurable down to 12 hours), so the queue stays small. No accounts, no sync, no cloud — everything stays on your Mac.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where do my links go when they expire?**
They're deleted from your Mac. There's no archive — Pannier is intentionally temporary, so the queue stays small.

**Can I change the expiry time?**
Yes. Open Settings from the popover → Queue → Links expire after. Anywhere from 12 hours to a week.

**Does Pannier sync between my Macs?**
No. Pannier is local-only by design. Each Mac has its own queue.

**Why do I need to copy the link first?**
Pannier reads from your clipboard when you hit the shortcut. Doing it that way means it works with any browser, any app, anything you can copy a URL from.

**How do I change the keyboard shortcut?**
Settings → Shortcut → Save link. Click the field and press a new combination.

**What about my privacy?**
Pannier doesn't collect any data. No analytics, no telemetry, no accounts. Everything stays on your Mac.
