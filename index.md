# Pannier

**Temporary Link Queue for Mac**

Pannier is a small menu bar app that holds links you'll get to later — a tab you'd otherwise leave open in Safari, an article to read tonight, a docs page you keep coming back to. Copy a link, hit ⌃⌘L, and it's saved. Open the menu bar icon to pick something to read.

Links auto-expire so the queue stays small. No accounts. No sync. No tracking. Everything stays on your Mac.

## Features

- **Menu bar resident** — Always one click away, never in your way
- **One-shortcut save** — ⌃⌘L grabs the link from your clipboard
- **Auto-expiring queue** — Configurable from 12 hours to a week
- **Local-only** — No accounts, no cloud, no sync
- **Search and filter** — Find anything in your queue instantly
- **Right-click actions** — Open, Copy URL, or Delete
- **Adapts to your system theme** — Light or dark, whichever you use
